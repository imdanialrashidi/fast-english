description: Turn a product idea into an evidence-backed product contract and staged roadmap
argument-hint: "<idea, problem, or opportunity>"
---

Develop this idea into a product contract:

$ARGUMENTS

Do not implement application code.

1. Read `AGENTS.md`, `docs/PRODUCT.md`, `docs/PLAN.md`, and relevant repository evidence.
2. Separate confirmed facts, assumptions, unknowns, and risky bets. Research only decisions that need current external evidence; prefer primary sources and record links.
3. Infer the target user, problem, business constraints, and MVP from repository/current evidence; state reversible assumptions and continue. Ask only when requirements conflict and no safe reversible interpretation exists.
4. Define the primary user, painful job, existing alternative, why now, desired behavior change, and measurable outcome.
5. Choose the thinnest end-to-end MVP that tests the riskiest product assumption. Reject feature-list planning.
6. Update `docs/PRODUCT.md` with must-have journeys, non-goals, constraints, acceptance, UX/performance budgets, and open product decisions.
7. Update `docs/PLAN.md` with evidence-based gates from discovery through production. Each phase needs an exit criterion and proof, not just a date.
8. Identify what must be learned through interview, prototype, analytics, technical spike, or production telemetry.

Return the product thesis, riskiest assumptions, MVP boundary, phase gates, first experiment, and exact documents updated.
