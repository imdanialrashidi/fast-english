# The Pyramids of Egypt

Welcome to this episode about the Pyramids of Egypt. The pyramids are among the most famous buildings in the world, and they have amazed travellers for thousands of years.

In this episode you will learn how the pyramids were built, who built them, and why they still stand today. The builders used huge blocks of stone and moved them across the desert with great care and skill.

Listen to the audio as you read. Try to repeat the sentences aloud and use the vocabulary list at the end of the lesson. The transcript is written for learners at the B1 level, so the words are simple and the sentences are short.

When you finish, you can check your understanding by looking at the example sentences in the vocabulary section. Good luck and enjoy the episode.
If you would like to continue practising, try writing a short summary of the episode in your own words and compare it with the vocabulary list. Repeating the key ideas in writing is one of the most effective ways to remember new language and to build confidence for the next episode.
