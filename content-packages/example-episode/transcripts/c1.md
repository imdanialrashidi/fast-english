# The Pyramids of Egypt

The Pyramids of Egypt represent one of the most remarkable achievements of ancient engineering. Constructed over a period of several centuries, these monuments continue to attract scholars and travellers alike.

Modern research suggests that the construction process involved thousands of skilled workers who quarried, transported and positioned enormous limestone blocks with remarkable precision. The alignment of the pyramids with the cardinal points remains a subject of ongoing archaeological debate.

This episode examines the social and economic organisation that made such construction possible, the religious significance of the monuments, and the ways in which later civilisations interpreted and reused them.
