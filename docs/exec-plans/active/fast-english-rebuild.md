Goal: rebuild Fast English from zero as a mobile-first Persian-RTL web product (Laravel 13 + Blade + Livewire 4/Alpine + Tailwind 4.1+ + Flux Free where needed + Filament 5 staff panel + PostgreSQL + Vite + Pest/Pint + Playwright) where learners pick a short English article at a CEFR level, read it, and hear the matching audio; with manual bank-transfer receipts, subscriptions, optional placement, PWA + online TWA, and staged slices S0→S9 (scope §1–3, §21).
